## What works
Details and how each was verified: archive § Done (Milestone 2), § Verified, and the ADRs.
- **Materials:** rows in `data/materials.json` (sand, water, oil, carrot, jelly, cork, dough; dough is the mochi class, ADR 0017), loaded, validated and packed by `cpu_ref/material_table.gd`; validator `tools/validate_materials.gd`. Parameter panel (top right) with sliders per class, a colour picker and reset; edits upload while the sim runs. Slider ranges stop where the GPU probe found an end unsafe: density 0.1–10, liquid density 0.5–2, drag 0–5, liquid drag from 0.1, body drag from 0.5, stiffness from 0.2 (ADR 0007, 0010).
- **Pool and hash:** a fixed cap (`config.json`) with a free-list; a spatial hash; the solver runs on the cell-sorted copy (SPEC 2.1).
- **Liquids:** PBF with s_corr and XSPH, wall density, a kick cap (`LIQUID_KICK` 2), drag per second (water 0.15), 2 substeps with a 4 px move cap per substep (480 px/s top speed), a lattice brush. Depth pressure comes from the density projection grid (`hydro.glslinc`, `hydro_solve`: 16 px cells, 32 red-black SOR sweeps in one workgroup, ghost-fluid free surface). Liquids layer by density (mass-weighted PBF and grid, ADR 0010).
- **Powder:** mass-weighted contacts with friction, `STACK_K` 0.3, inelastic push-out, sleeping only when held up and cradled, a lattice brush. Under water: sand sinks and settles, water leaves sand out of its density, a bed holds ~0.4 water per grain, the grid treats a sand bed and the floor as walls that hold the water's weight.
- **Bodies:** shape-matched cluster solids (ADR 0002) that float or sink by density and keep grains and water out (ADR 0003), collide and sweep fast impacts, turn a corner hit on another body into spin (ADR 0015), split under the eraser (ADR 0004), are never drawn inside each other, paused or not (archive § Bodies drawn into each other), bend over pair joints (jelly, ADR 0016) or regions (soft carrots or corks, ADR 0005), rest on each other (ADR 0008) and on sand at any stiffness (ADR 0009, 0013), and keep their dents with plasticity (ADR 0012; no shipped row has it).
- **Mochi (dough, ADR 0017):** no body and no rest shape; painted with the circle brush or "+10k dough". Each pair of one mochi material pushes where dense and pulls (by its cohesion, at half) where sparse, out to 4/3 spacings, equal and opposite (`mochi.glslinc`): a lump slumps into a mound in ~1 s and holds there (no creep, 0.003-0.03 px/s), stretches into strands that tear (26-30 px over a gap), pieces that touch merge (also after the eraser cuts it), and it holds a carrot dropped on it. Grains and bodies meet it as a grain. In water (ADR 0018), each mochi-water pair takes the pair rule's push with the water counted in the dough's density, so water lies at its own packing on a dough. Water closed in by dough is traded up out of it, and wet dough takes the grid's pressure push over its density, at most 1.25 gravities over it (ADR 0019: more held a dough dropped on water up like a lid, bouncing for ~20 s). "+10k dough" under "+10k water" keeps 5-13 water inside (269-305 before), and a dough of 0.9 floats, 1.0 hangs, 1.1 sinks. The grid pulls room beside a dough full as in open water (ADR 0020), so no air stays under liquid over a dough: "+10k oil" then "+10k dough" leaves 0.5-0.9% of columns with a 3+ px gap at 30 s (2.7-8.4% before, pockets up to 21 px at the walls). The dark line over the dough's top is also drawn over, as over sand. Dough round a pocket of liquid caught inside a dough takes no grid lift (ADR 0021: the liquid share of the 3 x 3 cells round it under 0.2-0.45), so a dough dropped through a pool no longer turns over round its pockets in bursts (fast dough 4-5x less, `doughfall` POP lines); a dough in open liquid floats, hangs or sinks as before. Sliders: density, friction, viscosity (slower flow), drag, cohesion (0 falls apart), adhesion; every end probed.
- **Stickiness:** `cohesion` and `adhesion` per material (ADR 0011): damp sand stands in columns, honey climbs walls.
- **Rendering:** 2×2 points into 640×360; liquids, then the gap over sand (`render_gap.glsl`), then powders; pore water shaded as wet sand.
- **Tools:** F3 overlay (time per pass, hash view, "GPU check"); `tools/gpu_probe.gd`, `tools/water_feel_probe.gd`, `tools/body_probe.gd` (`docs/agents/gpu-probe.md`); the game refuses to start on a stale shader import (`ComputeContext.import_is_stale`, SPEC 2.7).
- **Cost** (PC, RTX 3080 Ti, settled, 2 substeps, `--disable-vsync`, 2026-10-12): 0.62–1.14 ms a frame for 50k; mixed water and sand is the most expensive state (solve 0.7–0.8 ms), `hydro` 150–185 µs. Bodies pass ~40 µs for small bodies, ~219 µs for one 2000-member body (a 2000-member jelly: regions pass 510-600 µs; with pair joints, ADR 0016, about a third of what the regions build reads back to back); stickiness +60–70 µs at 50k while a material is sticky; plasticity 50–450 µs with a plastic soft body; mochi: a 2000-particle dough's frame 548-567 µs (a 2000-member jelly's 1261-1353 the same session), 10000 dough 593-621 µs (ADR 0017); 10k dough under 10k water 1084-1103 µs a frame, ~40 more than before ADR 0018. Phone: not measured. Tables: archive § Measured numbers.

## Known issues (open)
Read the entry and its archive section before tuning the solver: most obvious fixes were already tried.
- **A lopsided soft body creeps sideways on its own** (GPU `body_probe creep`, a tilted jelly at stiffness 0.2: 0.15 px/s, 0.046 before ADR 0013; none from 0.5 up). Cause: each member takes the plain mean of its regions' goals, so edge members count for more and the goals don't keep the body's momentum. FastLSM weights fix it (0.028 px/s), on main since 2026-10-17 for the owner to play: loaded soft bodies give more (a carrot dropped on a shipped jelly slid off in 2 of 3 runs), since the leak was holding loads up. Keep or revert (`git revert 63b2106`): ADR 0014. **Pair joints fix it without the softening** (ADR 0016, accepted 2026-10-17: owner, "joints are better"; the shipped jelly since 2026-10-17; dough is mochi since ADR 0017, so FastLSM serves no shipped row): creep 0.001 px/s, a carrot dropped 60 px stays on up to 4 px off centre (regions: only dead centre), `squash` 10% flatter (FastLSM 15-17%), and cheaper (a 2000-member jelly's frame 3.1 ms against 4.5). → archive § Lopsided soft body creeps sideways.
- **Dark strip: the air between settled sand and water is still in the sim.** The drawing covers it (`render_gap.glsl`; the owner looked on 2026-10-14: the strip is gone), and since ADR 0018 also over dough. Over dough the sim closes it since ADR 0020 (the grid's bubble pull beside mochi); beside sand the pull already runs, and the lids are held by water below them (archive). Tried: the grid push scaled by support below, box and ray occupancy tests, a particle-scale ghost-fluid pull, a smaller powder-liquid contact distance (0.9 × spacing closes most of it, at ~+30% water in a mounds bed: the owner's call). → archive § Dark strip between settled sand and water; Next item 1.
- **A wide floater is "a bit finicky"** (owner, 2026-10-15, after ADR 0003 item 18). Not looked into: ask before tuning it. A floater keeps ~1–3 px of air under it. → archive § Wide floater over a thin layer of air; ADR 0003 Known limits.
- **Liquids by density, left over:** a layered pool rocks for ~40–55 s; a thin dark line along the interface; from a fine mix 0.5–3% of the oil stays as a film on the floor (the measured fix changes pure water's floor push, so it waits for the owner); grains ignore a liquid's density, so sand sinks through liquid at 2 (owner: fine for now; a powder-rule change). → ADR 0010 Known limits; archive § Liquids ignore their material density.
- **Stickiness limits (ADR 0011 Known limits):** a sticky particle's stored velocity holds the next step's pull, so the probes' speeds overstate motion for sticky materials (read `rms_disp_per_sample`); at adhesion 1 splash can stay stuck high on a wall; a body doesn't stick to a side wall; a damp sand wall over ~15–20 rows tears.
- **Water over sand, left over:** a loop (≤6 px/s) in each bottom corner where a mound's toe meets the wall, an eddy over the V between two mounds (1.4–4.1 px/s), ~500 of 10k sunk grains creeping over 0.5 px/s. Tried for the corner loop: counting sand toward compression (brought the bowl loop back). → archive § Sand bowl, § Sand mounds, § Screen edges.
- **Body limits** (ADR 0002, 0003, 0005, 0008, 0012 and 0013 Known limits): a soft plank (0.5 and below) wedged into a gap is crushed at the supports' corners and turns partly inside out, which regional shape matching doesn't undo (at 0.2 it keeps tumbling the carrots; archive § ADR 0013 follow-ups; with pair joints, ADR 0016, 7 members against 12-25, and none at 0.5); big bodies are slow (the fused pass grows with the largest body; the split pass isn't scheduled, the 2000 brush cap stays: owner, 2026-10-14); a sunk body slides on a film of water; a big floater heaves with the pool; a long body takes ~15 s to rise; a cork buried under enough sand stays down; the push-out misfires at a rigid body's corners; a plastic soft body squeezes instead of spreading; bodies that end up inside each other never separate (soft ones tear into pieces hanging in the air; the brush no longer makes them, archive § Bodies drawn into each other).
- **Mochi dough limits** (ADR 0017 and 0018 Known limits): a lump holds only ~25-30 px of height and slumps to it in about a second (a stiffer pair rule jittered under Jacobi; a coloured solve is the lever); dropped from high onto loose sand it makes a crater (set down, it rests on top); in water a few particles still get caught at a hard landing (5-13 of 10k), its top under water is rougher, a "+10k dough" dropped on a pool still splashes for ~4 s and keeps 470-1090 water as specks in its top (ADR 0019), a dough bed under another dough jitters in place (99th percentile ~10-13 px/s, 0.06-0.28 px a frame, wet or dry; ADR 0019 Known limits), a 1.0 dough sinks slowly before it hangs, a 0.1 dough under a big pool turns over for a long time; since ADR 0020 (water pulled onto a dough's top) "+10k dough", "+10k water", "+10k dough" calms ~3 s later on average (9.8-18.3 s) and its second dough jitters 2-3x more at the end (0.18-0.22 px per 10 frames), and "+10k dough" then "+10k water" keeps 11-20 water inside; liquid caught in a dough dropped through a pool (490-1260 of 10k) stays, and a pocket of it now and then empties into a void that rises and bursts out of the top, weaker since ADR 0021 (the owner's call there stops it, at a cost to the sandwich); it sticks to other things only by adhesion (ADR 0011), weak beside its own pull; single particles hop while a lump creeps (10-25 px/s for a sample), so judge rest on the 99th percentile.
- `config.json` and `data/materials.json` are not auto-exported. The Android preset needs `*.json` in its non-resource include filter.
- Also open, moved to the archive (§ Known issues moved from STATUS): MCP runtime tools failing after headless runs, a headless `--import` in the main checkout dropping two `project.godot` lines (`git checkout project.godot`), sand under water somewhat compressed, the sleeping trade-off (slow grains on a slope stop), the top 20 px of 50k water at ~1.2 px/s, water drawn as plain 2×2 points (metaballs are M5), slow devices running in slow motion (intended), no keyboard shortcuts for materials, a sinking sand slab holding together ~1 s, the water brush pouring ~4× slower than before the lattice brush, gravity at 400 px/s² (a ~9 m tank; the owner's call).
