## Done (Milestone 2)
- **Material table:** `data/materials.json` holds sand (powder) and water (liquid).
  - Loaded, validated and packed into a GPU buffer by `cpu_ref/material_table.gd`.
  - Standalone validator: `tools/validate_materials.gd`.
- **Pool:** fixed cap from `config.json`, with a free-list (stack plus free count). Spawning can't exceed the cap, and nothing is deleted except by the eraser. CPU reference: `cpu_ref/particle_pool.gd`.
- **Spatial hash:** clear, count, a 3-dispatch prefix sum, then scatter.
  - The scatter copies particle state into cell order, and the solver runs on that sorted copy (SPEC 2.1).
  - Neighbour loops use each particle's hash cell, so pairs are symmetric (SPEC 2.7).
- **Liquid** (`gpu/shaders/sim/liquid.glslinc`): PBF density constraint, s_corr and XSPH viscosity, plus:
  - Wall density, so liquid doesn't crowd into the walls.
  - Density projection grid (`hydro.glslinc`, pass `hydro`, then `solve_pressure`). The free surface is a ghost-fluid boundary: p = 0 where the density crosses 0.5 between a water cell and its air neighbour (coefficient 1/θ, θ ≥ 0.1), air cells hold the extrapolated (negative) ghost pressure, and sampled p is clamped ≥ 0, so nothing pushes above the surface. Details of the grid: 16 px cells; liquid and powder particles splat their density with integer atomics, 32 red-black SOR sweeps (one dispatch each, warm-started) solve for a pressure p ≥ 0 that removes `HYDRO_K` 0.5 of each cell's compression and closes air bubbles under a full cell (sand counts toward full at `HYDRO_POWDER_FULL` 0.9 per unit of its density, since under water it packs to only ~1.1× water's rest density and water can't get into its pores; cells with sand fill ≥ 0.5 at `HYDRO_POWDER_VOL` 0.7 are a weight-bearing wall), and liquid particles are pushed by −∇p·dt² before the PBF iterations. It reaches the whole tank in one step, so deep water stays at rest density with gravity acting as usual.
  - Water drag 0.15 per second (`materials.json`, v × exp(−drag·dt)), so a slosh dies within about 10 s while falls and pours barely slow. Sand keeps the old 0.01 per step as 0.6 per second.
  - Substeps (`config.json` `substeps`, default 2): predict to velocity run twice per frame at dt/2 and the frame renders once. A move is still capped at `MAX_STEP` 4 px per substep, so the top speed went from 240 to 480 px/s. Drag, sleeping (`SLEEP_SPEED` 6 px/s), XSPH and `HYDRO_K` are per second or per 1/60 s, and `SCORR_K` (a position push standing in for a force) scales with dt², so none of them change with the substep count. Unscaled s_corr was 4× too strong at 2 substeps: the top layer of a resting pool drifted sideways at 3–7 px/s, up to 50 px/s (owner saw it); scaled, 0.35–0.6 px/s, max 2 (old code: 0.9, max 3).
  - Circle brush, liquids: new particles go only on free spots of a rest-spacing lattice (shifted each frame, checked against the last step's hash), up to the brush rate. Random spawn points used to land on each other and on existing water, and the solver blew them into a 300 px spray dome that drifted down. Pours are now a stream, but slower: about 930 particles in 3 s instead of about 4100, since new water fits only where the last has fallen away. Sand uses the same lattice (see Powder).
  - Kick cap (`LIQUID_KICK` 2, `velocity_update.glsl`): the solver's correction may stop water but add at most 2 × gravity × dt of speed per step in its own direction, so overshoot can't launch water.
- **Powder** (`powder.glslinc`, `solve_apply.glsl`, `velocity_update.glsl`): mass-weighted contacts with static/kinetic friction, averaged over contacts.
  - Height mass scaling (`STACK_K` 0.3), so piles aren't crushed under load.
  - Inelastic push-out (`MAX_SEPARATION` 0): impacts don't rebound grains, which removed the upward kicks at the edges.
  - Water yields to sand (`POWDER_LIQUID_SHARE` 0.1), and wet grains slip (`WET_SLIP` 0.9), so sand sinks through water and settles under it.
  - Floor friction, and Coulomb friction on the side walls.
  - Sleeping (`SLEEP_DISTANCE` 0.1 px) brings piles to rest; it fixed the pudding. A grain sleeps only when the solver holds it up (takes away at least half a step of gravity's speed), not on any change of speed: a falling grain moves 0.03 px in its first substep, under the sleep distance, so small groups whose contacts pushed sideways froze in mid-air (owner: erasing a brushed pile left clumps in the air; confirmed gone in play, 2026-10-11). It also must be cradled: touched below on both sides by grains (or a wall), or on the floor. A grain on a single grain counted as held up, so shafts cut with the eraser kept vertical walls and spikes (owner screenshot); now they cave in to a slope.
  - Circle brush (2026-10-10): sand goes on free lattice spots like water. Random points stacked new grains on each other and on the last frame's grains, and the inelastic push-out can't spring them apart, so a held brush built a packed blob that expanded up to the ceiling and hung there, shedding slow pairs (owner: "sand clumps in the air, against gravity"; confirmed gone in play, 2026-10-11). Grains that fall freely were never affected: sparse sand falls at free fall less drag 0.6/s.
- **Brush:**
  - Left mouse adds the selected material and right mouse erases any material.
  - Toolbar has Sand/Water, the two block fills ("+10k sand", "+10k water") and Clear.
  - Capacity meter turns red and reads FULL at the cap, and adding stops.
- **Rendering:** plain 2×2 colored points into a 640×360 texture.
- **Debug overlay (F3):**
  - GPU and CPU time per pass, averaged over 1 s, plus a TOTAL row.
  - Hash-grid view, which shades cells by particle count.
  - "GPU check" button: reads back the pool and hash once, verifies they agree, and reports the fullest cell.
- **GPU probe** (`tools/gpu_probe.gd`): fills, steps and measures the GPU sim from a real window. Usage is in its header.
- **Scenes:** `main.tscn` is the sim. The M1 smoke test is `debug/smoke_test.tscn`.

## Known issues
- **Water in a sand bowl circulates (owner, 2026-10-11: "water flows inside itself, underneath the surface, left and right"). Fixed on the probe and in `main.tscn` screenshots (on local main, not yet pushed; the owner played it before the mounds fix).** Measured with `water_feel_probe bowl` (see Verified).
  - Cause found: water counted sand in its PBF density. Grains pack to ~1.4× water's rest density, so water at a bed was always over-dense and pushed against it every step. Fixed: water leaves powder out of its density and fills the pores; the powder contact still keeps it off the grains. Water by the sand and the bed itself now come to rest. (commit 0830973)
  - Cause of the loop that was left (5.5–6 px/s): the density projection grid treated the sand bed as air. Pore water fills under 0.3 of a cell, so bed cells were air with p = 0 at the sand surface. `BOWLP` (grid pressure averaged from 30 s) showed it: open water was hydrostatic (+1.00 head per cell, as in the no-sand control), but p fell to ~0 toward the bed, e.g. 7.9 → 4.9 → 3.3 → 2.1 along one row toward the left slope, and 2.6 instead of 5.8 over the right shelf. The grid pushed water into the bed, and the contact push (summed, 90% to the water) threw it back out along the slope's normal, leaving gravity's share along the slope unbalanced.
  - Fixed: cells with powder fill ≥ 0.5 (`HYDRO_SOLID`) are a wall that holds the water's weight (dp/dn = ρg·n, not 0), and store the pressure continued hydrostatically into the pores (`hydro_sweep`). Alone: bulk 6.8 → 0.8–2.2 px/s gentle, 5.4 → 1.5–1.9 steep; what was left was an eddy over the slopes at up to 19 px/s in some runs. Then a liquid's contact push from sand is averaged over the grains it touches instead of summed (`contact_delta`): the grid holds water up at the bed, so the push only keeps water off the grains.
  - Side effects (owner's call): sand sinks through water faster again (settles in ~22 s, branch ~33 s, main ~21 s). Water in the sand bed: gentle 6.6–7.0k (was 5.7k), steep 4.6–4.8k (was 5.3k). The steep bowl's right mound now slumps into a slope once water reaches it, where on the branch it kept an overhang over the water. ~500 of 10k grains settled under water still creep over 0.5 px/s at 40 s (`sink`; branch 6), and sand moving in the bowls is 5–14 grains (was 0–7). In `sink` the water behind the sinking slab shows thin dark slivers near the surface for a few seconds (frame 900), closed by frame 1200.
  - Tried before the fix, not kept (bowl, plateau): sand as a solid wall in the grid (Neumann, cells with sand fill ≥ 0.5): bulk 7.7 (main 8.4–8.7). Sand weighted 0.7 in water's density: 7.8. Both: 6.5. Drag of water toward its sand neighbours (Darcy-like, 0.3 or 1.0 per 1/60 s): pore water 9.4 → 3.6 px/s, bulk unchanged. No grid push on water in sandy cells: worse (12). No bubble pull in sandy cells: no change. On top of the fix: drag, solid grid, or both: no better (5–8, within noise). Sand counted toward a liquid cell's fill when correcting compression: sinking sand settles as fast as main (~21 s, rafts 2283 vs 5913) and 10k sand in water keeps main's height, but water next to the bowl's sand moves at 4–5 px/s again and the loop stays. Water's contact push averaged over its contacts, or scaled to 0.3: loop halved (bulk 3.1) but 12–15k of the 27k water soaks into the sand. No kick cap: no change.
